## Product shape (as built)

Buyer-side capture only: the executing flow surface (the buying
agent's own runtime, or a Checkout-Hands-class surface) captures
the flow's default-state vector element by element at traversal
start and seals it; Darkseal diffs the sealed defaults against
the charges and terms that resulted, from the principal's own
confirmation/charge artifacts, under the published element
grammar (GRAMMAR.md, `darkseal-grammar/1`). Any third party
holding the sealed bundle + the grammar + the artifacts
recomputes the verdict with no service access.

- Element classes (bounded v1 set): checkbox · toggle ·
  radio/plan selector · continuity switch.
- Verdict classes (fixed, exhaustive): PRESELECTED-AND-CHARGED
  · PRESELECTED-NOT-CHARGED · NO-PRESELECTION · UNVERIFIABLE.
- Store field set is the spec §2 set only: no form-field
  values, no page bodies, no browsing history, no
  cross-principal aggregation.
- Separation (DK-C10): a checkout residue consisting solely of
  checkbox-class add-on defaults is marked UPSELLSEAL-OBJECT
  and mints no Darkseal whole-flow verdict.
- Pricing: FREE — the funnel shape. No metering event exists
  in the product; no paid tier alters verdict wording. This
  build adopts no price and creates no price point.
